Practical Info

Acquiring Art

Originals, limited prints, and art cards are available by individual request. Each work comes with a Certificate of Authenticity.

  • Originals: Oil on canvas, unique works. Shipped in protective crates.
  • Prints: Limited editions of 25 copies. Museum-quality archival printing. Delivered flat or in deep blue tubes.
  • Art Cards: Small formats that bring a fragment of art into everyday life. Available individually or in sets.

Shipping & Returns

  • International delivery via DHL or similar carriers.
  • Delivery time depends on region (typically 1–3 weeks).
  • Returns and cancellations follow current legislation.

Privacy & Data Protection

Personal data is used only for order processing and never shared with third parties.

FAQ

How can I order?

Via inquiry form or e-mail. We will guide you through the payment and shipping process.

 Are works framed?

All works are sold unframed to ensure safe transport and allow you to choose a frame that fits your space.

 How are works packaged?

Originals are shipped in custom-built wooden crates. Prints are delivered flat or rolled in heavy-duty protective tubes.

 How long does delivery take?

Typically 1–3 weeks, depending on the destination and customs processing for international orders.
