About the Artist

Ekaterina Rozenkrants — contemporary abstract expressionist painter.

Her painting unfolds as a space of states, transitions, and transformation. Within Frekvens 17, colour becomes voice and light becomes breath, shaping a style defined as energetic painting of transitions.

Lines and layers connect the inner and the outer, the visible and the invisible. Working in oil with multilayered techniques, the works create luminous depth, transparency, and resonance.

Each canvas is not a fixed form but a living movement that opens a field of inner connection for the viewer.
